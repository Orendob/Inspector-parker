INSPECTOR PARKER

— Logic Mysteries —

A guest of the manor has been murdered. Inspector Parker has rounded up the suspects and the constable has bagged the evidence. The rest is up to you — read the clues, eliminate the impossible, and accuse the killer.

Version 1

Original MVP

The first build. A 3 × 3 manor where each room already shows the suspect and weapon present. Find the murder scene from spatial and elimination clues.

  • 3 × 3 grid, 9 rooms, each visibly tagged with one suspect and one weapon.
  • Text clues with words like "north of" and "ruled out".
  • Tap a room to cycle blank → ruled out → suspected.

Version 2

Logic Grid + New Art

The proper deduction puzzle. Each room shows all four candidate suspects and weapons; cross out the impossibilities using pictographic clues. Whimsical-gothic portraits and full-color weapons.

  • 2 × 2 grid, scroll the manor sideways. Each room shows mini-icons of every candidate.
  • Picture clues: icon-symbol-icon chips. Tap a clue for a plain-English explanation.
  • Tap a mini-icon to cross it out as impossible. One suspect and one weapon per room.
