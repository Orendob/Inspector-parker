INSPECTOR PARKER

— Logic Mysteries —

A guest of the manor has been murdered. Read the picture clues, cross out the impossibilities, and unmask the killer. Fifty cases per difficulty — every player gets the same level N.

Easy
How to Play
1

One per room

The manor is a grid of rooms (2 × 2 on Easy, 3 × 3 on Hard). Each room holds exactly one suspect and one weapon, and every suspect and weapon is in one and only one room. Find who's where with what.

Each cell has one suspect + one weapon. Always.
2

Tap the picture clues

Picture clues live under the manor. Tap a clue chip to read what it means in plain English and to make every place those items appear glow on the map.

↓
The suspect's room is directly above the weapon's room.
Tap a chip → tooltip + cyan glow on every matching room.
3

Cross out impossibilities

Every room starts with several candidate suspects and weapons shown as mini-icons. Tap a mini-icon to cross it out when a clue rules it out. The remaining (uncrossed) item is the answer for that room.

Inside one room — only one of these can really be there:
Cross out the impossible ones · the one still glowing is the answer.
4

Accuse the killer

When you've narrowed it down, tap Accuse, pick the crime scene, the killer, and the weapon. Get all three right and the manor erupts in confetti and applause — and your solve time is saved as your best.

ACCUSE
Three right answers → confetti + applause + best-time saved.
5

Clue chip cheat-sheet

Read every chip as: from the left item, go (arrow) to reach the right item.
