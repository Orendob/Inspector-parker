    How to Play

    A guest of the manor has been murdered. Use the clues to deduce who did it, with what, and where.

    • The manor has 9 rooms in a 3 × 3 grid. Each room contains one suspect and one possible weapon.
    • One of these rooms is the crime scene. The suspect there is the murderer; the weapon there is the murder weapon.
    • Read the clues carefully — some reference spatial relations (rows, columns, adjacency, direction).
    • Tap a room to mark it: blank → ✕ ruled out → ○ suspected → blank.
    • When ready, tap Accuse and pick the murderer, weapon, and room. All three must be correct.
