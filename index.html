    How to Play

    A guest has been murdered in the manor. There are 4 suspects, 4 weapons, and 4 rooms. Each room holds exactly one suspect and exactly one weapon — and each suspect and each weapon is in exactly one room. Figure out who, where, and with what.

    • Each room initially shows all 4 candidate suspects and all 4 candidate weapons.
    • Read the clues and tap mini-icons to cross them out as impossible. Tap again to undo.
    • Because each room has exactly one of each, the moment you confirm "X is in room A" you can also rule out X in every other room and rule out every other suspect/weapon in room A.
    • When only one suspect and one weapon remain in each room, you've solved the placement.
    • Tap Accuse and pick the killer, weapon, and room.
